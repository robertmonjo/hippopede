\section*{Justification}

\paragraph{Nils Schön\-eberg.}
Cited in the manuscript for the 2024 BBN baryon-abundance update
(JCAP 2024, 006). Expert in primordial nucleosynthesis, Hubble tension,
and CMB constraints on non-standard expansion. Directly qualified to
evaluate the Appendix on projected thermal asymptotics and the
BBN-motivated limit $\alpha=1/2$.

\paragraph{Mohammed Koussour.}
Co-author of Myrzakulov et al.\ (2025, Nuclear Physics B), cited for
the observational values of $q_0$ and $z_t$ used in the comparison
figures. Active in cosmographic parameter estimation and modified gravity
(f(Q), f(Q,T) theories). Qualified to assess the kinematic observational
section and the projected-hippopede fit to chronometer data.

\paragraph{Javlon Rayimbaev.}
Co-author of the same Myrzakulov et al.\ (2025) paper. Publishes
actively on modified gravity, relativistic astrophysics, and cosmological
constraints in Elsevier journals, including Physics of the Dark Universe.
Qualified to evaluate the model's embedding construction and its
comparison with standard and modified-gravity backgrounds.

\paragraph{Jian-Ping Hu.}
First author of Hu et al.\ (2025, MNRAS 542, 1063--1075), cited for
the screened transition-redshift estimate $z_t = 0.64\pm0.16$.
Co-author of Gao et al.\ (2024, MNRAS), cited for the FRB+SNe
cosmographic value $q_0=-0.50\pm0.20$. Expert in $H(z)$ compilation,
cosmic chronometers, and Hubble-tension diagnostics. Qualified to
assess the data section and the kinematic observational comparison.
